Analyst takeaways

What the numbers are saying

01

Top-line stabilization

Revenue was essentially flat in FY2026 at $46.4B after falling roughly 10% in FY2025. That suggests the decline paused, but the business remains well below the FY2024 revenue peak.

02

Margin recovery is modest

Gross margin improved to 42.9% in FY2026 from 42.7% in FY2025, but it remains 170 basis points below FY2022's 46.0% level.

03

Cash generation weakened

Operating cash flow fell from $7.43B in FY2024 to $2.87B in FY2026, a decline of roughly 61%. That is the most important deterioration in the five-year view.

04

Profitability remains under pressure

Net income declined from $5.70B in FY2024 to $3.11B in FY2026. FY2026 net margin was approximately 6.7%, versus 11.1% in FY2024.

Methodology

Built from reported financial statements

Figures are reported in Nike's fiscal years ending May 31 and are shown in U.S. dollars. Revenue, net income, gross margin, diluted EPS, and operating cash flow come from Nike's SEC Form 10-K filings. Calculated ratios such as net margin and operating cash flow as a percentage of revenue are derived from those reported figures.

This is an educational portfolio project and not investment advice. All analysis is the author's interpretation of public filings.
